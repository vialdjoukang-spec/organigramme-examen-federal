Recent evidence supports placing the large, over-the-needle catheter at the fifth interspace, slightly anterior to the midaxillary line

ATLS recommendations have changed in 2018 from the 2 nd intercostal space in the midclavicular line (ICS2-MCL) to the 4 th /5 th intercostal space just anterior to the mid axillary line (ICS 4/5-MAL)
