# Définition de la rétention aiguë. La conduite du sondage suisse n'est pas dans cet extrait.

Acute retention of urine is defined as a painful, palpable or percussible bladder, when the patient is unable

to pass any urine [3].

• Chronic retention of urine is defined as a non-painful bladder, which remains palpable or percussible after the patient has passed urine. Such patients may be incontinent [3].

• Bladder outlet obstruction is the generic term for obstruction during voiding and is characterised by increasing detrusor pressure and reduced urine flow rate. It is usually diagnosed by invasive urodynamic or

pressure/flow studie [3].
