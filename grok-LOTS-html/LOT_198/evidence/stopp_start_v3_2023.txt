O'Mahony D, Cherubini A, Guiteras AR, Denkinger M, Beuscart JB, Onder G, et al.
STOPP/START criteria for potentially inappropriate prescribing in older people: version 3.
European Geriatric Medicine. Published 31 May 2023. Open access. Article lu le 1er novembre 2026.
URL : https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41999-023-00777-y

STOPP/START version 3 has been expanded and validated by an international European panel of experts in geriatric pharmacotherapy. Version 3, with 190 criteria, is significantly larger than version 2 (114 criteria).

The panel achieved consensus on 133 STOPP criteria and 57 START criteria, i.e., 190 STOPP/START criteria in total representing a 66.7% increase in the number of criteria compared to STOPP/START version 2 published in 2015.

The first version of STOPP/START in 2008 included 65 STOPP and 22 START criteria. STOPP/START version 2 in 2015 was expanded to include 80 STOPP PIM criteria and 34 START PPO criteria.

STOPP criteria for detection of potentially inappropriate medications are based on physiological systems, with additional categories relating to patients at risk of falls, patients taking opioid analgesics and patients taking drugs with anticholinergic properties. START criteria detect potential prescribing omissions, also arranged according to physiological systems.

Delphi: each statement rated on a 5-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 (strongly agree) to 5 (strongly disagree). Agree = 2, neutral = 3, disagree = 4.
Rules for acceptance: (i) a median Likert value of 1 or 2, and (ii) a 75th centile Likert scale value that was not greater than 2.0.

Three of the 114 version 2 criteria were judged obsolete and removed:
STOPP C2: Aspirin with a previous history of peptic ulcer disease without concomitant PPI (risk of recurrent peptic ulcer).
STOPP H7: COX-2 selective NSAIDs with concurrent cardiovascular disease (increased risk of myocardial infarction and stroke).
START B1: Regular inhaled beta-2 agonist or antimuscarinic bronchodilator for mild to moderate asthma or COPD.

Literature window for new criteria: April 2014 to 31 March 2022.
Eleven physicians from 8 European countries. Four Delphi rounds.
Round 1: 183 of 204 proposed criteria (89.7%) accepted.

The number of STOPP criteria has increased from 80 in version 2 to 133 in version 3, an 66.25% increase. START from 34 to 57, a 67.6% increase. Overall from 114 to 190, a 66.7% increase.
Version 1 is also described as 87 criteria.

Recent analysis of the specific STOPP and START criteria elicited in the OPERAM trial shows that over half of all the STOPP and START recommendations generated by the software tool were not considered clinically relevant in individual patients.

The same systems-based design as the two previous versions was maintained for version 3.
