Asrani SK, Kim WR. Organ allocation for chronic liver disease: model for end-stage liver disease and beyond. Current Opinion in Gastroenterology. 2010 May;26(3):209-213. DOI 10.1097/MOG.0b013e32833867d8. Extrait lu le 31 octobre 2026.

MELD = 9.57 × ln(creatinine, mg/dl) + 3.78 × ln(total bilirubin, mg/dl) + 11.2 × ln(INR) + 6.43. Lower limits of the components are bound by 1 and creatinine is capped at 4 mg/dl.
MELD-Na = MELD − Na − [0.025 × MELD × (140 − Na)] + 140. Serum sodium is bound between 125 and 140 mmol/l.
MELD was originally developed to predict survival in patients undergoing elective TIPS, then used for liver allocation from 2002.
