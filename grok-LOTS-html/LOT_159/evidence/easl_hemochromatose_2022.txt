EASL Clinical Practice Guidelines on haemochromatosis. Journal of Hepatology, publié en juin 2022. DOI 10.1016/j.jhep.2022.03.033. Extraits lus le 31 octobre 2026.

In patients homozygous for p.Cys282Tyr in HFE, provisional iron overload based on serum iron parameters (transferrin saturation >45 percent and ferritin >200 µg/L in females, and transferrin saturation >50 percent and ferritin >300 µg/L in males and postmenopausal women) is sufficient to diagnose haemochromatosis. Other HFE genotypes require hepatic iron overload on MRI or liver biopsy. The first step is transferrin saturation and serum ferritin. A transferrin saturation >50 percent in men and >45 percent in women has been defined as elevated. Phlebotomy targets: ferritin <50 µg/L during induction and <100 µg/L during maintenance. Samples for transferrin saturation should be taken in the morning; fasting does not improve diagnostic utility.
