EASL Clinical Practice Guidelines: Autoimmune hepatitis. Journal of Hepatology. Reçu le 30 juin 2015, publié en ligne le 1er septembre 2015. Extraits lus le 31 octobre 2026.

The simplified IAIHG score (2008) is based on four parameters: autoantibodies, serum IgG, typical or compatible histology, and absence of viral hepatitis. Definite autoimmune hepatitis: at least 7 points. Probable: at least 6 points. Compared with the 1999 score, sensitivity 95 percent versus 100 percent, specificity 90 percent versus 73 percent, accuracy 92 percent versus 82 percent. In adults, significant immunofluorescence titers are at least 1:40. In children, titers of 1:20 for ANA or SMA and 1:10 for anti-LKM1 are supportive. IgG is increased in approximately 85 percent of patients. About 25 percent present acutely. In acute onset, 25 to 39 percent have normal IgG and 9 to 17 percent lack autoantibodies. Paris criteria for AIH-PBC variant: at least two of three for each disease. PBC: ALP at least 2 times ULN or GGT at least 5 times ULN; AMA; florid bile duct lesions. AIH: ALT at least 5 times ULN; IgG at least 2 times ULN or SMA; moderate or severe interface hepatitis. Induction uses steroids and thiopurines are added as a steroid-sparing strategy. Laboratory endpoints are normalisation of IgG and ALT.
