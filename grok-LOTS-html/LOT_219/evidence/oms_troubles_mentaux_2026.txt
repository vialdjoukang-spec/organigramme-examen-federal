Nearly 1 in 7 people in the world live with a mental disorder. Mental disorders can severely disrupt daily life and cause long-term suffering if left untreated. There are many different types of mental disorders. Effective prevention and treatment options exist. Most people do not have access to effective care. A mental disorder is characterized by a clinically significant disturbance in an individual’s cognition, emotional regulation, or behaviour. It is usually associated with distress or impairment in important areas of functioning. There are many different types of mental disorders.
