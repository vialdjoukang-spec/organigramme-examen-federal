Organisation mondiale de la santé. Glossaire, WHO recommendations for care of the preterm or low-birth-weight infant, 2022. Consulté le 31.10.2026.

Low birth weight: birth weight below 2.5 kg.
Very low birth weight: birth weight below 1.5 kg.
Extremely low birth weight: birth weight below 1 kg.
Term gestation: born at 37 0/7 to 41 6/7 weeks of gestation.
Preterm: born before 37 0/7 weeks of gestation.
Very preterm: born before 32 0/7 weeks of gestation.
Extremely preterm: born before 28 0/7 weeks of gestation.
Post-term: born at or after 42 0/7 weeks of gestation.
Corrected age: chronological age minus the number of weeks or months born preterm, with term defined as 40 weeks for the calculation.
Postmenstrual age: age counted from the first day of the mother's last menstrual period.
