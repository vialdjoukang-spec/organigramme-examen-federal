Early fetal growth restriction is distinguished from late fetal growth restriction at 32 weeks. Congenital anomalies should be absent for the diagnosis of placental fetal growth restriction.

Early, before 32 weeks, solitary parameters:
- abdominal circumference below the 3rd centile
- estimated fetal weight below the 3rd centile
- absent end-diastolic flow in the umbilical artery

Early contributory parameters: abdominal circumference or estimated fetal weight below the 10th centile combined with pulsatility index above the 95th centile in the umbilical artery or in the uterine artery.

Late, from 32 weeks, solitary parameters:
- abdominal circumference below the 3rd centile
- estimated fetal weight below the 3rd centile

Late contributory: at least two among estimated fetal weight or abdominal circumference below the 10th centile, crossing centiles by more than two quartiles, and cerebroplacental ratio below the 5th centile or umbilical artery pulsatility index above the 95th centile.

Abnormal functional parameters alone do not diagnose late fetal growth restriction if fetal size is not compromised.
