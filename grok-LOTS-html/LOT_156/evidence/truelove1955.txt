« Severe.—Severe diarrhoea (six or more motions a day) with macroscopic blood in stools. Fever (mean evening temperature more than 99.5 F. (37.5 C.), or a temperature of 100 F. (37.8 C.), or more on at least two days out of four). Tachycardia (mean pulse rate more than 90 per minute). Anaemia (haemoglobin 75% or less). E.S.R. much raised (more than 30 mm. in one hour). »
« Mild.—Mild diarrhoea (four or less motions a day) with no more than small amounts of macroscopic blood in stools. No fever. No tachycardia. Anaemia not severe. E.S.R. not raised above 30 mm. in one hour. »
