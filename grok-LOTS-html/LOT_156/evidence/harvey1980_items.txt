1. General well-being the previous day: 0 very well, 1 slightly below par, 2 poor, 3 very poor, 4 terrible.
2. Abdominal pain the previous day: 0 none, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 severe.
3. Number of liquid stools per day: score 1 per movement.
4. Abdominal mass: 0 none, 1 dubious, 2 definite, 3 definite and tender.
5. Complications, score 1 per item: arthralgia, uveitis, erythema nodosum, aphthous ulcers, pyoderma gangrenosum, anal fissure, new fistula, abscess.
