Table 1.3 lue le 1er novembre 2026. Seuils dans la meilleure oreille, moyenne à 500, 1000, 2000 et 4000 Hz.
Normal hearing : less than 20 dB.
Mild hearing loss : 20 to less than 35 dB.
Moderate hearing loss : 35 to less than 50 dB.
Moderately severe hearing loss : 50 to less than 65 dB.
Severe hearing loss : 65 to less than 80 dB.
Profound hearing loss : 80 to less than 95 dB.
Complete or total hearing loss/deafness : 95 dB or greater.
Unilateral : less than 20 dB in the better ear, 35 dB or greater in the worse ear.
The classification and grades are for epidemiological use and applicable to adults.
While audiometric descriptors provide a useful summary of an individual's hearing thresholds, they should not be used as the sole determinant in the assessment of disability or the provision of intervention(s) including hearing aids or cochlear implants.
