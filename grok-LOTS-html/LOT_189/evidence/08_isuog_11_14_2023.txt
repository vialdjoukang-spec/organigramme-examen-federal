ISUOG Practice Guidelines (updated): performance of the 11-14-week ultrasound scan. Ultrasound in Obstetrics and Gynecology. Première publication le 3 janvier 2023. DOI 10.1002/uog.26106. Extrait déjà archivé dans le dépôt et relu le 1 novembre 2026.

A routine scan at 11 + 0 to 14 + 0 weeks confirms viability and plurality, dates the pregnancy, screens aneuploidy, identifies major structural anomalies and screens preterm pre-eclampsia. When several crown-rump lengths are taken, gestational age uses the best-quality measurement between 45 and 84 mm. Nuchal translucency above 3.5 mm leads toward invasive testing in the pathway described before cell-free DNA. Cell-free DNA detected 99.7% of trisomy 21 at a 0.04% false-positive rate, 97.9% of trisomy 18 and 99.0% of trisomy 13 in the series cited. The mixture model had 90% sensitivity for 97% specificity for trisomy 21. Heart activity, situs, axis 30 to 60 degrees, four chambers, outflow tracts and three-vessel view are listed. Tricuspid regurgitation is flow above 60 cm/s for more than 50% of the cycle. Nuchal translucency is increased in up to 40% of fetuses with a major cardiac abnormality.
