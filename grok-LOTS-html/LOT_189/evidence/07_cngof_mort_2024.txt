Fetal death: expert consensus of the French College of Gynecologists and Obstetricians. International Journal of Gynecology and Obstetrics. 2024. DOI 10.1002/ijgo.16079. Page lue le 1 novembre 2026.

In France, the prevalence of fetal death after 22 weeks is between 3.2 and 4.4/1000 births. Fetal genetic testing identifies a genetic abnormality in 7.2% to 18.5% of cases. At diagnosis, Kleihauer-Betke and irregular agglutinin tests are recommended systematically. Hemostatic evaluation is recommended in retroplacental hematoma, hypertensive disease, or sepsis. A postnatal sample from the placental fetal surface is recommended. In the absence of disseminated intravascular coagulation or a maternal life-threatening emergency, the woman's wishes about timing are considered, return home is possible, and the preferred mode is vaginal. The consensus recommends mifepristone at least 24 hours before induction. The milligram figures of that consensus are not copied into the lesson: the Swiss professional information is used for the dose. Active fetal movement assessment: look for heart activity, growth restriction and anemia, record the heart rate and movements, or scan movements and fluid, without requiring a Manning score. Fetal heart-rate monitoring is not indicated in the next pregnancy solely because of a prior fetal death.
