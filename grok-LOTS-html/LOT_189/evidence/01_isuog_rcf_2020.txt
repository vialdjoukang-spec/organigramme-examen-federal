Extraits lus.

The most common definition of SGA is EFW or AC below the 10 th percentile of given reference ranges. Fetal size alone is not sufficient to identify FGR, unless AC or EFW is below the 3 rd percentile. A drop in fetal growth velocity, i.e. drop in AC or EFW of > 2 quartiles or > 50 percentiles, should alert the physician to possible FGR.

Table 2. Early FGR: GA < 32 weeks, in absence of congenital anomalies: AC/EFW < 3 rd centile or UA-AEDF. Or AC/EFW < 10 th centile combined with UtA-PI > 95 th centile and/or UA-PI > 95 th centile. Late FGR: GA ≥ 32 weeks: AC/EFW < 3 rd centile. Or at least two out of three of the following: AC/EFW < 10 th centile; AC/EFW crossing centiles > 2 quartiles on growth centiles; CPR < 5 th centile or UA-PI > 95 th centile. The authors of this ISUOG guideline recommend the Delphi consensus criteria for definition of FGR.

32 weeks seems to be the optimal gestational-age cut-off at diagnosis. Around 70% of women with early FGR will develop hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, mainly pre-eclampsia. For extreme prematurity (< 27 weeks) and extremely low birth weight (< 600 g), each day of prolongation of the pregnancy improves neonatal survival by 2%.

Delivery: at any gestational age, maternal indication or obstetric emergency. 24 + 0 to 25 + 6 weeks: personalized management. From 26 + 0 weeks, deliver if spontaneous repeated persistent unprovoked fetal heart rate decelerations, or altered BPP (score ≤ 4). 26 + 0 to 28 + 6 weeks: ductus venosus a-wave at or below baseline or STV < 2.6 ms. 29 + 0 to 31 + 6 weeks: ductus venosus a-wave at or below baseline or STV < 3.0 ms. 32 + 0 to 33 + 6 weeks (permitted after 30 + 0 weeks): UA-EDF reversed or STV < 3.5 ms. ≥ 34 + 0 weeks (permitted after 32 + 0 weeks): UA-EDF absent or STV < 4.5 ms. Corticosteroid prophylaxis is recommended if delivery is planned before 34 + 0 weeks. Elective Cesarean delivery is recommended if abnormal cCTG STV, ductus venosus Doppler alteration, absent or reversed UA-EDF, altered BPP, or maternal indication.

Magnesium sulfate prophylaxis: there is good evidence for fetal neuroprotection before preterm delivery, but the optimum gestational-age threshold is not uniform. The guideline recommends referring to local or national guidelines. MCA Doppler seems to guide monitoring before 32 weeks but there is no evidence that it should be used to determine delivery timing. After 34 + 0 weeks, the median interval between a low MCA-PI and stillbirth was ≤ 5 days. Loss of fetal tone is associated with cord pH < 7.00 or a base excess < −12 mEq/L. Loss of fetal gross body movement with ductus venosus alteration can predict cord pH < 7.20.
