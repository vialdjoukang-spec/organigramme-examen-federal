Consensus definition of fetal growth restriction: a Delphi procedure. Gordijn et al. Ultrasound in Obstetrics and Gynecology. 2016. DOI 10.1002/uog.15884. Page lue le 1 novembre 2026.

For early FGR (< 32 weeks), solitary parameters were abdominal circumference (AC) < 3 rd centile, estimated fetal weight (EFW) < 3 rd centile and absent end-diastolic flow in the umbilical artery. Contributory parameters were AC or EFW < 10 th centile combined with a pulsatility index (PI) > 95 th centile in either the UA or uterine artery. For late FGR (≥ 32 weeks), solitary parameters were AC or EFW < 3 rd centile. Contributory parameters were EFW or AC < 10 th centile, AC or EFW crossing centiles by > two quartiles, and cerebroplacental ratio < 5 th centile or UA-PI > 95 th centile.
