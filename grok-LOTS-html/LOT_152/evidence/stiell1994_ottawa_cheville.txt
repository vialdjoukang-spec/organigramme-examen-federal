La validation de 1993 (Stiell et al., JAMA 1993;269:1127-1132, PMID 8433468) rapporte une sensibilité de 1,0 pour les fractures de la zone malléolaire et du médio-pied après affinement.

Texte de la fiche :
« An ankle x-ray series is only required if there is any pain in malleolar zone and any of these findings: bone tenderness at A (posterior edge or tip of lateral malleolus) OR bone tenderness at B (posterior edge or tip of medial malleolus) OR inability to bear weight both immediately and in ED.
A foot x-ray series is only required if there is any pain in midfoot zone and any of these findings: bone tenderness at C (base of 5th metatarsal) OR bone tenderness at D (navicular) OR inability to bear weight both immediately and in ED.
Do not use for patients under age 18. Clinical judgement should prevail if the patient is intoxicated or uncooperative, has other distracting painful injuries, has diminished sensation in the legs, or has gross swelling which prevents palpation. »
