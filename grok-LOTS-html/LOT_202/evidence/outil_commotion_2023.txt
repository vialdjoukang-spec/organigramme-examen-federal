Outil d'évaluation de la commotion liée au sport, version de 2023, décrit dans le consensus d'Amsterdam. Extrait lu le 1er novembre 2026.

Previous versions of the SCAT have been shown to be most effective in discriminating between concussed and non-concussed athletes within 72 hours of injury and up to 5-7 days postinjury. Except for the symptom scale, these tools may not be appropriate for use in the return-to-sport decision-making process beyond 7 days postinjury.

The Child tool should be used in patients aged 8-12 years. A duration of 10 to 15 minutes was not found in the archived excerpt and is not taught.
