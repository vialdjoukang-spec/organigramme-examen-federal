« Type 0: normal small-intestinal mucosa with less than 30 IELs per 100 enterocytes.
Type 1: normal villous and crypt architecture and an increased number of IELs (≥30 per 100 enterocytes).
Type 2: normal villous architecture and crypt hyperplasia with ≥30 IELs.
Type 3a: mild villous atrophy, villous to crypt ratio <3:1 or 2:1, ≥30 IELs.
Type 3b: marked villous atrophy, ratio <1:1, ≥30 IELs.
Type 3c: total villous atrophy, flat mucosa, ≥30 IELs.
Type 4: flat mucosa with few crypts and near normal IEL counts, rare, seen in refractory sprue. »
