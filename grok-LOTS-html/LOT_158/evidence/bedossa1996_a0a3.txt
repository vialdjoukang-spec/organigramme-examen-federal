Extrait lu le 31.10.2026. Bedossa P, Poynard T, METAVIR Cooperative Study Group. An algorithm for the grading of activity in chronic hepatitis C. Hepatology 1996;24:289-293. DOI 10.1002/hep.510240201.
Légende de la figure 1, page lue :
« Chronic hepatitis C with no activity (A0). Portal tract with inflammation but no piecemeal nor lobular necrosis.
(B) Chronic hepatitis with mild activity (A1). Piecemeal necrosis is graded 1 (focal alteration of the periportal plate), and lobular necrosis is graded 0.
(C) Chronic hepatitis with moderate activity (A2). Piecemeal necrosis is more diffuse (grade 2), and there are foci of lobular necrosis (grade 1).
(D) Chronic hepatitis with severe activity (A3). Piecemeal necrosis is graded 2, and there are several necroinflammatory foci per lobule (grade 2). »
Le texte nomme aussi : A0 no histological activity, A1 mild activity, A2 moderate activity, A3 severe activity.
