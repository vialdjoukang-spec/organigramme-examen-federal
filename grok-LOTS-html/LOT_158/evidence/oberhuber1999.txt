The histopathology of coeliac disease: time for a standardized report scheme for 
pathologists.

Oberhuber G(1), Granditsch G, Vogelsang H.

Author information:
(1)Department of Clinical Pathology, University of Vienna, Medical School, 
Austria. Georg.Oberhuber@akh-wien.ac.at

In this paper, we review the histological features of coeliac disease and 
propose a standardized report scheme based on the Marsh classification. 
Furthermore, terms used by pathologists are defined. The most important 
histological differential diagnoses are given, as well as a definition of the 
different clinical forms of coeliac disease such as symptomatic, silent, latent, 
potential, treated and refractory coeliac disease.
