Grossman JM, Gordon R, Ranganath VK, et al. American College of Rheumatology 2010 recommendations for the prevention and treatment of glucocorticoid-induced osteoporosis. Arthritis Care Res (Hoboken). 2010;62(11):1515-1526. Guide clinicien et article lus le 31.10.2026.

A rapid decline in bone mineral density begins within the first 3 months of glucocorticoid use and peaks at 6 months.
For postmenopausal women and men age 50 years or older starting glucocorticoid therapy with an anticipated duration of at least 3 months, or prevalent glucocorticoid therapy of at least 3 months: low-risk patients, alendronate, risedronate or zoledronic acid for 7.5 mg/day prednisone or more. Medium-risk patients, alendronate or risedronate for any dose, zoledronic acid for 7.5 mg/day or more. High-risk patients, alendronate, risedronate, zoledronic acid, or teriparatide.
The clinician guide states an increased fracture risk with dosages of prednisolone or equivalent as low as 2.5 to 7.5 mg daily.
