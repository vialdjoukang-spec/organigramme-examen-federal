Practice Point 1.3.1: Routine evaluation of urine sediment for erythrocyte morphology and the presence of red cell casts and/or acanthocytes is indicated in all forms of glomerular disease.
Practice Point 1.3.2: Monitoring of hematuria (magnitude and persistence) may have prognostic value in many forms of glomerular disease. This is particularly applicable to immunoglobulin A nephropathy and immunoglobulin A vasculitis.

Nephrotic syndrome is defined as proteinuria >3.5 g/d plus hypoalbuminemia (<30 g/l), often, but not necessarily accompanied by dyslipidemia and edema.
Nephrotic-range proteinuria is stated as >3.5 g/d. Nephrotic-range proteinuria without the nephrotic syndrome is distinguished from the full syndrome.
