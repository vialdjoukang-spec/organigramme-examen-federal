American Diabetes Association. Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026, section 6, glycemic goals and hypoglycemia.
Texte lu dans le PDF des Standards 2026 (Diabetes Care 2026).

Level 1 hypoglycemia is defined as a measurable glucose concentration <70 mg/dL (<3.9 mmol/L) and ≥54 mg/dL (≥3.0 mmol/L).
Level 2 hypoglycemia (defined as a blood glucose concentration <54 mg/dL [<3.0 mmol/L]).
For most individuals, 15 g carbohydrates should be ingested.
Individuals should be counseled to recheck their glucose 15 min after ingesting carbohydrates.
Individuals using automated insulin delivery systems should typically ingest 5–10 g carbohydrates.
