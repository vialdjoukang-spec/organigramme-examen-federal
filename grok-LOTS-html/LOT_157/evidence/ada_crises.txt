Section 16, Diabetes Care in the Hospital, critères diagnostiques de l'acidocétose et de l'état hyperosmolaire.
Page lue : https://diabetesjournals.org/docm-care/article/1/3/533/164627/Section-16-Diabetes-Care-in-the-Hospital
Le tableau est adapté de Umpierrez GE et al. Hyperglycemic crises in adults with diabetes: a consensus report. Diabetes Care 2024;47:1257–1275.
Les Standards of Care 2026 renvoient à ces tableaux 16.1 et 16.2 (DOI 10.2337/dc26-S016).

DKA: Glucose ≥200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) or prior history of diabetes.
Ketosis: β-Hydroxybutyrate concentration ≥3.0 mmol/L or urine ketone strip 2+ or greater.
Metabolic acidosis: pH <7.3 and/or bicarbonate concentration <18 mmol/L.
HHS: Plasma glucose ≥600 mg/dL (33.3 mmol/L).
Calculated effective serum osmolality >300 mOsm/kg (2×Na + glucose) or total serum osmolality >320 mOsm/kg (2×Na + glucose + urea).
All of the criteria listed must be met. One-third of hyperglycemic emergencies have a hybrid DKA-HHS presentation.

Complément lu le 31 octobre 2026 sur la même page hospitalière et dans les Standards 2026.
Initiate or intensify insulin for persistent hyperglycemia starting at a threshold of ≥180 mg/dL (≥10.0 mmol/L), confirmed on two occasions within 24 hours, for the majority of noncritically ill individuals.
Blood glucose before, during, and after surgery should be maintained between 100 and 180 mg/dL (5.6 and 10.0 mmol/L).
Hold metformin and other oral glucose-lowering agents on the day of surgery.
Discontinue SGLT2 inhibitors 3–4 days before elective surgery.
Recommendation 16.14 of the 2026 Standards suggests an A1C goal <8% (<64 mmol/mol) within 3 months of elective surgery.
