A fixed rate intravenous insulin infusion (FRIII) calculated on 0.1 units/per kilogram body weight is recommended.
When glucose drops below 14 mmol/L, consider reducing the rate of intravenous insulin infusion to 0.05 units/kg/hr.
Potassium replacement: potassium level > 5.5 mmol/L : nil.
Potassium level 3.5-5.5 mmol/L : 40 mmol/L of infusion solution.
Potassium level < 3.5 mmol/L : senior review, additional potassium required.
No potassium with the initial fluid resuscitation if the serum potassium remains above 5.5 mmol/L.
