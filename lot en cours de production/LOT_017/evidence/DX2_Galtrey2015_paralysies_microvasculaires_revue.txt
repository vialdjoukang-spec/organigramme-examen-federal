RÉSUMÉ (mot pour mot) : « Patients with isolated unilateral pupil-sparing third or isolated fourth or sixth nerve palsies over 50 years are often diagnosed with "microvascular extraocular palsies". This condition and its management provoke divergent opinions. We review the literature and describe the incidence, pathology, clinical presentation, yield of imaging, and management. A retrospective diagnosis of exclusion has little practical use. We suggest a pragmatic approach to diagnosis, investigation, and management from initial presentation. »
