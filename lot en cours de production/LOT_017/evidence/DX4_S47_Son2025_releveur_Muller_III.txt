« The levator palpebrae superioris is the primary retractor muscle responsible for the elevation of the upper eyelid. »
« It is a skeletal muscle innervated by the superior division of the oculomotor nerve. »
« The Müller’s muscle—a smooth muscle with sympathetic innervation—arises from the underside of the levator palpebrae superioris and is inserted into the superior tarsal plate. »
« It helps the levator palpebrae superioris to raise the upper eyelid, and is responsible for approximately 1–2 mm of the additional elevation ( 4 , 8 ). »
« It carries the motor nerve fibers to some of the extraocular muscles (levator palpebrae superioris, superior rectus, medial rectus, inferior rectus, and inferior oblique muscles). »
