« Given that the oculomotor nerve in the subarachnoid space of the basal cisterns is in close proximity to the posterior communicating artery aneurysm (PcomA) arising from internal carotid artery, the majority of ONP-causing aneurysms are PcomAs (over 80%) [ 6 ]. »
« Due to anatomical features, 20%–32% of PcomAs may contribute to ONP [ 9 , 10 ]. »
« Among all causes of acquired ONP, aneurysms account for 6%, while in individuals over 60, aneurysm-related ONP makes up about 11% [ 1 ]. »
« PcomA-induced ONP is most prevalent among individuals aged 40–60, similar to the age range for intracranial aneurysms [ 15 , 16 , 17 ]. »
« However, two multicenter, large-sample studies showed that women accounted for approximately 84% of PcomA-induced ONP cases, a proportion that seems to exceed the percentage of women among all intracranial aneurysm patients [ 9 , 16 ]. »
« It is adjacent to the junction of the internal carotid artery and the posterior communicating artery, running slightly below and parallel to the posterior communicating artery, with a distance of about 1.7 mm between them. »
« In a cross-section of the oculomotor nerve, parasympathetic fibers are located in the superficial layer of the nerve trunk [ 24 , 25 ]. »
« Therefore, when the oculomotor nerve is compressed, pupil changes are theoretically the first to occur. »
« This is because the fibers innervating the pupil are located in the superior-medial superficial layer of the oculomotor nerve [ 27 , 28 ]. »
« In the vast majority of PcomA-induced ONP cases (approximately 98.6%), the pupil is affected, typically presenting with dilated, poorly reactive pupils, although occasionally constricted or oval-shaped pupils may occur [ 6 ]. »
« Early compression of the oculomotor nerve may cause irritation, explaining the rare occurrence of constricted pupils. »
« More than 50% of patients with unruptured PcomA and ONP experience ipsilateral periorbital pain [ 11 , 21 , 30 , 31 ]. »
« The exact mechanism remains unclear, but three hypotheses may explain it: (1) Local dural irritation: The tentorium cerebelli has recurrent branches from the ophthalmic division of the trigeminal nerve. »

« However, DSA is invasive, with an estimated risk of neurological and systemic complications ranging from 1% to 2%, which is higher in the elderly or those with cerebral arteriosclerosis [ 28 , 34 ]. »
« According to the literature, the smallest PcomA causing ONP is at least 4 mm in size, which can be detected by current noninvasive imaging modalities such as CTA or MRA [ 36 , 38 , 39 ]. »
« It is estimated that the misdiagnosis rate of PcomA-induced ONP, based on the ONP classification system, is no less than 10%. »
« Therefore, all patients with ONP should be advised to undergo CTA or MRA as soon as possible to quickly determine the cause of ONP. »
« Surgical clipping is preferred in younger patients (under 60 years old), those with ONP symptoms longer than 7 days, an aneurysm size ≥7 mm, or complete ONP. »
« In contrast, endovascular therapy is recommended for older patients, those in poor health, or undergoing treatment with antithrombotic agents. »
