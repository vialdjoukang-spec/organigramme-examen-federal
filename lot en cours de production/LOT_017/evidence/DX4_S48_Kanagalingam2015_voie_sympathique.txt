« 3 ( Figure 1 ) Localization Central (first-order neuron) Horner syndrome First-order neurons are located in the posterolateral hypothalamus, and from there, sympathetic fibers pass through the lateral brain stem and extend to the ciliospinal center of Budge and Waller in the intermediolateral gray column of the spinal cord at C8–T1 ( Figure 2 ). »
« 18 – 20 Preganglionic (second-order neuron) Horner syndrome The preganglionic sympathetic neurons exit from the ciliospinal center of Budge and Waller and pass across the pulmonary apex. They then ascend through the stellate ganglion and up the carotid sheath to synapse at the superior cervical ganglion, located at the level of the bifurcation of the common carotid artery and the angle of the jaw. »
« 48 Postganglionic (third-order neuron) Horner syndrome The postganglionic (third-order) sympathetic neurons originate in the superior cervical ganglion, travel in the wall of the internal carotid artery, and continue on to the cavernous sinus. »
