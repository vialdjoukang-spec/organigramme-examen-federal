During starvation, body stores of phosphate decrease, despite normal serum levels. As long as the energy metabolism depends on fat oxidation, phosphate is not required; as soon as the patient resumes carbohydrate intake, the metabolism of glucose uses large amounts of phosphate, thus leading to a drop in serum levels [ 12 , 15 , 64 ].
