EXTRAIT (mot pour mot) :
This chapter provides a set of generic situations covering the common circumstances, symptoms, complaints and findings which the physician should be able to manage on day one of his or her residency. 
In other words, he or should be able to assess a patient presenting with any of these situations in a well-structured way, establish a differential diagnosis, and propose diagnostic, therapeutic, social and preventive/counselling measures. The list encompasses most of the typical situations a young resident may face. The situations are listed under several subheadings to make their use easier, but in some instances, the classification is arbitrary. Items listed as “symptoms” may also be defined as “findings”, and vice-versa. A set of pre-defined criteria was used to design the list. The situations were selected:
if they occur frequently 
if rapid and appropriate intervention may be crucial or even life-saving 
if they are a recognized cause of important emotional distress for the patient
                    1. General complaints and symptoms (G)

SSP G15 : weight gain, obesity
SSP G16 : weight loss, loss of appetite

SSP S3.1 : abdominal pain
SSP S3.8 : diarrhea

SSP S9.4 : change in eating behavior
SSP S9.5 : change in mood

SSP F2.9 : abnormal glycaemia and markers of glycaemia homeostasis
SSP F2.14 : abnormal thyroid hormones
SSP F2.16 : anemia
