... f an asthma attack (particularly if treated in hospital) has long been recognised as a risk factor for future asthma attacks and death. However, the NRAD found that among people who died from asthma, 10% had recently been discharged from hospital following treatment for an attack, and that 21% had attended an emergency department at least once for asthma in the year before they died. Clearly systems are required that ensure post-asthma attack reviews are performed by appropriately trained clinicians. The purpose of the review is to determine what led to the attack, assess the patient's current asthma control and future risk factors, and to optimise treatment. The NRAD panellists commented on the poor quality of medical records, in particular a lack of detailed recording of asthma reviews, the assessments made and any action taken.
