         without sustained ROSC with ongoing CPR to a PCI centre for
         consideration for angiography or ECPR, depending on available
         resources and team expertise.

Toxins

    Ensure your personal safety, as direct skin contact (e.g. mouth-to-mouth
    ventilation) might transmit toxic agents.
    Assess all patients in cardiac arrest for potential poisoning.
    Reduce absorption, consider using specific treatment measures as antidotes,
    decontamination and enhanced elimination.
    Administer antidotes, where available, as soon as possible.
