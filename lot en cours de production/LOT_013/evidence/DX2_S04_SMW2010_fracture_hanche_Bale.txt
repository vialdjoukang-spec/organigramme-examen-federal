         Mortality
         Mortality for the overall sample during the initial acute
         hospital stay was 2.6%. Mortality one year after the frac-
         ture for the overall sample was 22%. Only nine patients
         (3%) could not be reached and were lost for the follow-up,
         probably because they died, lived abroad, or could not be
         reached for another reason. One year mortality in patients
         who were community dwelling pre-fracture was signific-
         antly lower than for patients who lived in a nursing home
         pre-fracture (12% vs. 44%, p < .001).
