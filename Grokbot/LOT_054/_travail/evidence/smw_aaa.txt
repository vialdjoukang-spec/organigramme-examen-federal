Swiss Medical Weekly 2022. Hospital incidence and mortality of patients treated for abdominal aortic aneurysms in Switzerland. Secondary analysis of Swiss Federal Statistical Office discharge data, years 2009-2018. A total of 10,728 cases were included, of which 87.1% were male. Overall, 22.7% of the patients presented with a ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm; 46% of these cases were surgically treated whereas 54% received conservative therapy. The age-standardised cumulative hospital incidences for treatment of nonruptured abdominal aortic aneurysms were 2.6 and 19.7 per 100,000 for women and men. For ruptured aneurysms it was 0.4 per 100,000 in women, and 2.7 in men. The adjusted mortality rates for treatment of nonruptured aneurysms decreased from 5.5% in 2009 to 1.4% in 2018 in women, and from 2.4% in 2009 to 0.6% in 2018 in men. The adjusted mortality rates for treatment of ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysms remained high: for women 32.4%, for men 19.7%. EVAR is used in up to 80% of elective cases and in up to 60% of ruptured cases in several European countries. Open aneurysm repair was performed in more than 60% of the patients presenting with rAAA in Swissvasc. The proportion of cases of nrAAA treated with EVAR increased from 46.5% in 2009 to 67.0% in 2018. The proportion of cases of rAAA treated with EVAR increased from 29.1% to 44.3%. Of the 2430 cases presenting with rAAA, 46.2% underwent surgery. 33.1% at university hospitals. 56.3% at other major hospitals. 10.6% in small hospitals. 97.3% of rAAA treatments were performed at major hospitals. 37.4% were referred from regional hospitals.
