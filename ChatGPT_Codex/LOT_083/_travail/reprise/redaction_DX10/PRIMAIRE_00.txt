#### Overview

## BSR's 'gold standard' clinical guidelines support evidence-based clinical practice in rheumatology.

Our guidelines are developed using the methods and processes outlined in [**Creating Clinical Guidelines: British Society for Rheumatology Protoco** l](https://www.rheumatology.org.uk/Portals/0/Documents/Guidelines/BSRGuidelinesProtocol_Guidance_v6.1_Oct2026.pdf?ver=q8CWKjo-Z7WUAfXAtZbt3Q%3d%3d) and overseen by our Guidelines Steering Group. Our guidelines grow out of the collaborative efforts of many members and non-members, specialists and generalists and people with lived experience of the topic being covered. We also involve [**trainees**](https://www.rheumatology.org.uk/Portals/0/Documents/Guidelines/BSR%20GWG%20member%20trainee%20role%20description_v1.1.pdf?ver=s8bZzAtCPsAx3jdxSPRemA%3d%3d) in our guideline working groups as an important educational opportunity. **To** **express an interest in joining a guideline working group** **, complete our** **[online form](https://forms.monday.com/forms/4ede8756315ed9f85f88664ff71af0fe?r=use1)** **.** All guidelines are published in _ Rheumatology _ under an ‘open access’ licence. To develop our guidelines, we partner with the University of Keele to support the evidence review process. Listen to our recent [podcast episode](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1972502/episodes/14848545) with guideline working group members for tips on applying to join.
