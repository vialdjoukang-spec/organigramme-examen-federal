Among all patients whose low back pain had a specific, clinically relevant cause, 4% were diagnosed with disk herniation, 3% with spinal stenosis, and 2% with spondylolisthesis.

Roughly 1–4% of patients were found to have a vertebral body fracture on their primary investigation; 0.7% had a tumor (primary or metastatic), 0.2% had ankylosing spondylitis, and 0.01% had spondylodiscitis
