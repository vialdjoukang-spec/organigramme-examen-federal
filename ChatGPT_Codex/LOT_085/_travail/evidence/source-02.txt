Except for patients with signs of sepsis, empirical antibiotic treatment should await diagnostic sampling of joint fluid to avoid false negative culture results.

No blood tests have either the sensitivity or the specificity to confirm or exclude SANJO.

aspiration of synovial fluid should be performed as quickly as possible when SANJO is suspected

Nevertheless, the presence of crystals does not rule out SANJO
