Evaluation of MINOCA syndrome and HEART score in patients presenting to the emergency department with panic attack and chest pain complaints. Taslidere B, Atsiz A. Ir J Med Sci 2023;191(5):2351-2356. Patients presenting with chest pain may or may not be experiencing a panic attack. Patients who applied to the emergency department with panic disorder and chest pain complaints were included. The study was completed with 143 eligible patients out of a total of 217 patients evaluated. Patients who met the MINOCA diagnostic criteria were identified. While the mean HEART score was 4.7 in the MINOCA group, it was 2.0 in the non-cardiac group, a statistically significant difference. Clinicians should pay attention to the patient's age, gender, number of attacks per week, HEART score, and which symptoms (palpitations, shortness of breath, and fear of death) are present in patients who meet the panic attack diagnostic criteria. Clinicians should be alert to the MINOCA syndrome in panic attack patients.
