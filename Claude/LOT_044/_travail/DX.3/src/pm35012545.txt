Anxiety disorders in patients with noncardiac chest pain: association with health-related quality of life and chest pain severity. Hamel S et al. Health Qual Life Outcomes 2022;20(1):7. Patients with noncardiac chest pain (NCCP) report more severe symptoms and lowered health-related quality of life when they present with comorbid panic disorder (PD). A total of 915 patients with NCCP were consecutively recruited in two emergency departments. Comorbid PD or GAD are prospectively associated with increased chest pain severity and lowered health-related quality of life in patients with NCCP.
