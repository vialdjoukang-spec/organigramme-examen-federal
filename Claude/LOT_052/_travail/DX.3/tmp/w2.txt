w0ce40c-146 Topic 2: Non-operative management of uncomplicated acute appendicitis Q.2.1: Is non-operative management with or without antibiotics a safe and effective treatment option for adult patients with uncomplicated acute appendicitis? Recent systematic reviews and meta-analyses of RCTs have concluded that the majority of patients with uncomplicated AA can be treated with an antibiotic-first approach [ 16 , 18 , 100 ].
